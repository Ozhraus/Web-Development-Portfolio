Active

ping: send ICMP packet; particularly interesting are ICMP echo/type 8 and ICMP echo reply/type 0.
tracert: hop lookup.
telnet: remote unencrypted connection to systems. Can be used as netcat (client-type) to establish unecrypted connection to whatever server.
netcat (nc): supports TCP/UDP as client or server.
