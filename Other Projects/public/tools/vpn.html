Virtual Private Network (VPN)

Similarly to NAT for definition and SSH for concept, a VPN is a technology that allows to establish a secure tunnel within an insecure network, providing confidentiality, integrity, authenticity and a simulated LAN access. Its functioning is grounded on IPSec stack protocol, a Layer 3 protocol that provides all the VPNs characteristics and feature.

How does a VPN work

A VPN works by creating an encrypted tunnel between:

  • Routers (Site to Site): so communication is tunneled within Internet
  • Client-Router (Remote): communication is encrypted from client to its VPN site destination
